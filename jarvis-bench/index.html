JARVIS-Bench

Benchmarking Personal Intelligence Agents on Long-Horizon Real-User Daily Traces

Real people logged 28 to 44 days of their own lives. Can an AI agent tell what they prefer, when to step in, how they feel, and whether preferences carry over to a new domain?
